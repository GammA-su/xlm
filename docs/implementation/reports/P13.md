### 3.4 Presets, diffs and gating (`sources/mix01.py`)

`MixturePreset` validates the pack recipes: weights must sum to **exactly** one
(`Fraction` over the decimal spellings, never float tolerance), silent
renormalization and non-`error` missing-source policies are rejected at parse
time, and every named component must resolve to a declared view. `diff_presets`
produces the M0–M5/no-IFM structured diffs. `mix01_status` reports per-component
READY / BLOCKED / NOT LIVE-VERIFIED; `gate_preset_for_run` raises
`Mix01BlockedError` on anything but READY and never substitutes the no-IFM
alternative. `PilotAuthorization` is an explicit opt-in envelope (operator flag,
source allowlist, document/byte caps); the pilot itself is NOT RUN.
